The catalog contains 289 products, 289 configurations, 28 published product records and 17 manually reviewed affiliate offers. The latest batch deepens nine existing Finnleo records with exact model-page evidence for seating capacity, exterior dimensions, materials where stated and electrical requirements. The preceding batches added six sauna tents, six current ThermaSol variants and four Harvia Utu cabins, and corrected model-level data for Finnmark FD-1, FD-3 and FD-5. FD-5 is classified as hybrid because its exact product record documents both full-spectrum infrared and a traditional stone heater.

## Evidence-depth gaps

- 91 configurations still lack a documented seated capacity.
- 149 configurations still lack complete exterior dimensions.
- 128 configurations still lack model-level materials.
- 205 configurations still lack a documented supply voltage.
- 148 products remain below seven documented core fields in the internal ten-point completeness view.
- The largest low-depth brand groups are Finnleo, Almost Heaven Saunas, Cedarbrook Sauna + Steam and Harvia.

## Next data priorities

1. Complete the remaining Finnleo records, prioritizing the models with the most complete technical documentation. Nine records now have a deeper evidence bundle; the other Finnleo candidates still need model-level review.
2. Replace range-level Almost Heaven placeholders with exact capacity, exterior dimensions, included heater and electrical-package facts.
3. Normalize the remaining Finnmark FD-2, FD-4, FD-6 and FD-7 records. The catalog now has one verified hybrid record, but the other combination models still need exact model pages.
4. Define portable-specific review fields for tents. Stove inclusion, fuel, chimney diameter, hot-zone clearances and seated capacity should not be inferred from camping-berth counts.
5. Treat publication as a separate gate. New records remain candidates until their model-specific editorial copy, source review and presentation have passed QA.
