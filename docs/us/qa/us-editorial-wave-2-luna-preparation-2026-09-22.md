# US editorial wave 2 — Luna preparation

Date: 2026-09-22

## Prepared cohort

Sixteen candidate products now meet the technical gate and have product-specific, source-bound editorial copy. The cohort contains outdoor sauna kits and indoor or outdoor sauna cabins from Redwood Outdoors, Finnleo, SunRay, Salus, Tylo and GeyserSteam.

The products remain candidates. Their pages are not public or indexable until the separate Sol review checks normalized facts, source alignment, copy quality, links, metadata, build output and desktop/mobile presentation.

## Corrections made before copy preparation

- Renew King is classified as hybrid, but Salus states both 8 kW / 40 A and 6 kW / 30 A on the same official page. Its electrical facts are stored as a conflict and the product is held.
- Deluxe Panorama specifies two separate 50 A breakers. The former aggregate 100 A scalar was removed because it described neither one breaker nor the heater's rated current. The product is held until split circuits can be modeled safely.
- Hekla, Lukaku, Autana and Valera use a traditional heater in the reviewed base configuration. Infrared or red-light systems are optional add-ons and are no longer marked as included.
- Autana is held because the official heading says six to eight people while the specification table says four to six.
- Balerion is confirmed by the current page title and specifications. Its Shopify URL retains an older handle, which is recorded as a locator note rather than treated as a second model.

## Corrections after the first Sol review

- NorthStar 5 × 6 is held because the same official model page says both that the cabin seats three and that its seating capacity is two. Both statements remain preserved as a conflict.
- NorthStar 5 × 7 keeps the manufacturer's four-to-five-person range in its evidence and editorial copy. Five remains only the documented upper bound used by capacity filtering.
- Finnleo page slugs are no longer exposed as manufacturer SKUs. The reviewed pages do not label those slug strings as SKUs.
- Horizon and Summit retain the manufacturer's listed weights only in the raw source record. The values are not normalized as net weight because the page does not identify the weight type.
- Breaker, connection-type and dedicated-circuit fields are now independent. Ampere values are no longer copied into breaker fields, and hardwired no longer implies a dedicated circuit.
- Lulea's included chromotherapy and optional Bluetooth sound bar are separate component records.
