## Corrections made before copy preparation

- Renew King is classified as hybrid, but Salus states both 8 kW / 40 A and 6 kW / 30 A on the same official page. Its electrical facts are stored as a conflict and the product is held.
- Deluxe Panorama specifies two separate 50 A breakers. The former aggregate 100 A scalar was removed because it described neither one breaker nor the heater's rated current. The product is held until split circuits can be modeled safely.
- Hekla, Lukaku, Autana and Valera use a traditional heater in the reviewed base configuration. Infrared or red-light systems are optional add-ons and are no longer marked as included.
- Autana is held because the official heading says six to eight people while the specification table says four to six.
- Balerion is confirmed by the current page title and specifications. Its Shopify URL retains an older handle, which is recorded as a locator note rather than treated as a second model.
