# Sol review: Finnmark catalog batch 11

## Result

The batch remains unpublished and contains no new affiliate offers. The model identities and hybrid classification are coherent, but two source corrections were required before acceptance.

## Corrections

- FD-2 retailer-derived dimensions and 2,400 W power were replaced with the official Finnmark specification sheet. The reviewed record now stores 48 × 44 × 78 in exterior dimensions, 43 × 40 × 70 in interior dimensions and a 120 V / 1,750 W / 15 A NEMA 5-15P infrared supply.
- FD-4 was normalized to the official Finnmark sheet. Its interior dimensions are 44 × 45 × 75 in, while the traditional heater is documented as 120 V / 1.9 kW / 20 A.
- Phase, breaker size, required circuit rating and dedicated-circuit status remain unknown where the manufacturer sheet does not label them explicitly.
- FD-6 and FD-7 retain model-level retailer evidence and candidate status. Their separately sold 4.5 kW heaters do not justify inferred voltage, current or circuit values.
