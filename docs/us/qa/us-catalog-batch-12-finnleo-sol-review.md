# Sol review: Finnleo catalog batch 12

## Result

The four model identities, dimensions, materials, heating components and documented voltage values match the official Finnleo model pages. The records remain candidates and add no affiliate offers.

## Corrections and limits

- The IS440 page states that the sauna plugs into a 120 V household outlet. It does not explicitly state that a dedicated circuit is unnecessary, so dedicated-circuit status remains unknown.
- Solace preserves Finnleo's 4–5 seated range in evidence, stores the documented maximum 5 for filtering and records the stated two-person reclining capacity.
- Twilight preserves the 4–5 seated range in evidence, stores the documented maximum 5 for filtering and records the stated capacity of up to three reclining occupants.
- Hallmark 44 and IS440 keep reclining capacity unknown because the reviewed model pages do not state it.
- Breaker size, required circuit rating, phase and plug type remain unknown unless the model page explicitly states them.
